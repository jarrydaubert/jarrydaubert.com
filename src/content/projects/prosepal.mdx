The card you keep putting off. The message that needs a little more thought. ProsePal gives you a starting point to make your own.

I'm rebuilding it in SwiftUI, exploring on-device generation and cloud AI where appropriate. The aim is a more natural iPhone experience, with room to read, adjust and choose your words.

The [existing App Store app](https://apps.apple.com/app/prosepal/id6757088726) is the earlier Flutter version. This SwiftUI rebuild isn't released yet.
